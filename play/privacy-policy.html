encrier — privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

Encrier is a handwritten note and task app. Everything you write stays on your device.

  • No data collection. Encrier does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no accounts.
  • All data is local. Your handwriting, tasks, sketches, and corrections are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, and are removed if you uninstall the app.
  • Network use. The app's only network activity is a one-time download of the on-device handwriting-recognition model through Google Play services. Handwriting recognition itself runs entirely on the device; your ink never leaves it.
